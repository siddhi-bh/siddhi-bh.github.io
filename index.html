About

I like catching trouble before it becomes a headline

Hi, I'm Siddhi, a defender at heart. I love the detective side of security: reading logs, spotting the one odd login in a sea of normal ones, and figuring out what an attacker was trying to do. Think of me as the person who notices the unlocked window before anyone else does.

I build my own detection tools to practise, from a phishing URL detector to an intrusion detection project and a threat hunting dashboard. Time on the offensive side taught me how attackers think, and I use that to defend better. I'm growing toward a SOC analyst role and I'm happy to keep learning from every alert I triage.
